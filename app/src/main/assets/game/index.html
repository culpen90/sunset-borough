How to play

On foot

Push the left stick up to walk where you’re looking or down to backpedal. Hold Sprint to run. On the right side of the view, swipe left to turn left or right to turn right as you walk. Lift your thumb to hold that view.

Stand near a car and tap Enter or Take car. Tap Strike to throw a punch.

At the wheel

Hold Gas and use the arrows to steer. Use Brake for tight turns or Reverse to back up.

Follow the gold marker for jobs. Tap the radar for a street map. Lose pursuing patrols to clear your stars.

Adaptive cruise

Win Boulevard Run to unlock adaptive cruise. Tap Set cruise on or beside a street. Cruise steers you into a forward lane and holds your current speed, or 20 km/h if you’re going slower. At a junction, aim toward the street you want. Steer at any time and release the arrow for a quarter second to let cruise center the car again.

Use − / + to adjust speed and Gap to leave one, two or three car lengths behind traffic. Cruise slows for yellow when it can stop before the line, waits on red, and resumes on green. Hold Gas to accelerate temporarily. Brake, reverse, or tap cruise again to cancel.
